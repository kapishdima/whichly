How it works

Four steps. No new tools.

01

Wrap variants in data attributes.

Add data-vp-block and data-vp-variant to the sections you want to compare. Plain HTML — no framework wrapper.

02

Drop in one script tag.

Add <script src="cdn.optio.dev/optio.js"> with your project ID. 39KB, async, zero blocking.

03

Share the staging URL.

Send the link to your client. They open it, see the picker in the corner, click through the variants — no login required.

04

See the choice in your dashboard.

Each pick lands in app.optio.dev with comments and context. Ship the chosen variant on the next deploy.
